## Logo

The mark is a bento box whose compartments hold simple UI tiles (play, toggle, card) — "UI
composition" made literal. Variants on the board: primary logo (icon + wordmark + tagline),
app icons (1024 down to 64 px, light and dark), a geometric wordmark, and monochrome
simplified marks (black, gray, white-on-dark).
